# Frequently Asked Questions

**Can you tell me exactly how many units are left?**
We don't share exact stock counts. The assistant can tell you whether an item is available for the quantity you want; if it can't confirm, the team follows up with you personally.

**Can you look up someone else's order?**
No. For privacy we never share any information about other customers or their orders.

**How do I check the status of my own order request?**
A team member contacts you within 1 business day using the contact details you gave in chat. You can also email support@loomhaus.example with your name.

**Do you offer gift wrapping?**
Yes — recycled kraft gift wrap and a handwritten note for $4. Ask the team when they confirm your order.

**Where are your products made?**
Hoodies and tees are cut and sewn in Portugal, denim in Japan, knitwear in Scotland, and totes in the USA.

**How does sizing run?**
Hoodies are relaxed (size down if between sizes); tees and denim fit true to size. The beanie is one size.
