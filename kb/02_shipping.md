# Shipping Policy

- Processing time: orders are packed within 1–2 business days after the team confirms payment.
- Standard shipping (US): 3–7 business days, $5.95 flat rate. Free standard shipping on orders over $75.
- Express shipping (US): 1–3 business days, $14.95. Not available to PO boxes.
- Canada, United Kingdom and European Union: 7–14 business days, $19.95 flat rate. Import duties and taxes may be charged by the destination country and are the customer's responsibility.
- We do not currently ship to other countries.
- Every order ships with tracking; the tracking link is emailed when the parcel leaves the warehouse.
- Lost or delayed parcels: contact support if tracking hasn't updated for 7 business days.
