# Pricing, Payments & Promotions

- Prices shown on product pages are final. Loomhaus does not negotiate prices.
- The chat assistant and the support team cannot create discount codes, apply discounts, change prices, price-match, or waive shipping fees. There is no "admin mode" in chat.
- Promotions are only ever announced through the official Loomhaus newsletter, and promo codes are entered by the customer at checkout.
- We don't offer price adjustments after purchase.
- Accepted payment methods: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. Payment happens on our secure checkout page, never in chat. We will never ask for your card number in chat.
