# Returns & Exchanges

- You can return unworn, unwashed items with original tags within 30 days of delivery.
- Exchanges (US): free — we email a prepaid return label and ship the replacement once the return is scanned.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after we inspect the return. Original shipping costs are not refunded.
- International returns: the customer pays return shipping.
- Final sale: socks and anything marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned, for hygiene reasons.
- Damaged or defective items: contact support within 14 days of delivery with a photo and we'll replace or refund, including shipping.
