# Loomhaus — Store Overview

Loomhaus is a small independent online store selling everyday apparel and accessories built to last: heavyweight hoodies, tees, denim, knitwear and bags. We ship from our warehouse in Portland, Oregon.

- Customer support: chat on our website, or email support@loomhaus.example
- Support hours: Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm Pacific. Chat requests outside those hours are answered the next business day.
- How ordering through chat works: when you tell our assistant you want to buy an item, we record an order request and reserve the item for you. A Loomhaus team member contacts you within 1 business day to confirm size, colour, payment and shipping. You are never charged in chat.
- If we can't immediately confirm an item for the quantity you want, we still record your request and a team member follows up with you personally (restock timing or alternatives).
